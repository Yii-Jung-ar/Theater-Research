## ③ Spatial Relationship Description

* The main theatre volume is embedded within a larger public building envelope.
* Public circulation wraps around the auditorium, allowing visitors to move through foyers while maintaining separation from backstage functions.
* The building organizes a strong distinction between public front-of-house and performer/service back-of-house areas.
* Multiple circulation routes connect the city, waterfront, roof landscape, and interior foyer system.
* The auditorium acts as the primary spatial anchor around which supporting functions are arranged.
* Exterior roof surfaces function as an extension of the public realm and connect urban circulation directly to the building.

---

## ④ Overall Spatial Logic

The project is organized as a monumental performance volume embedded within an accessible public landscape. Rather than separating architecture and public space, the building transforms its roof into a continuous urban surface connecting the city, waterfront, and cultural program. The theatre volume forms the organizational core while circulation layers create a gradient from public to highly controlled backstage spaces.
